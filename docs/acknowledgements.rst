Acknowledgements
================

Tradewell, Cazzaniga, Pagani and colleagues not only published their two
azoospermia models with the coefficients in full, they deposited the
development and both external validation cohorts under a CC0 license on
Harvard Dataverse. That is rare in clinical prediction research, and it is
what makes an independent implementation checkable: every number in these
docs can be rerun from the public data with ``make data_gen preprocess``.

``seminal`` is based on the models in the studies listed in
:doc:`references`. Please refer to them for a full account of how the models
were developed and validated.

``seminal`` is an independent implementation and is not affiliated with or
endorsed by the original authors or their institutions.
