The Data

The study data are public under CC0 on Harvard Dataverse (doi:10.7910/DVN/EQFMCM): one Excel file with a sheet per site (Miami 1,070 rows, Milan 1,955, Chicago 643) and a key. The repository never commits data; data/ is gitignored.

Download and harmonize

make data_gen      # python data_gen.py      -> data/raw/      (one Parquet per sheet)
make preprocess    # python preprocessing.py -> data/processed/tradewell_2022.parquet

data_gen.py only fetches and saves. preprocessing.py renames each site’s columns to the five model inputs, applies the exclusions, builds the outcome, labels the testis volume method per site, median-imputes LH, testosterone and testis volume within site, and adds the FSH / LH / testosterone / volume categories used in the paper’s Table 1. Options:

--exclusions reproduced|stated   which exclusion rule (default reproduced)
--no-impute                      leave missing hormones as NaN
--csv                            also write a CSV copy
--raw-dir / --out-dir            paths

The output has one row per man with site, the five inputs, azoospermia (0/1), included (passes the exclusion rule), testis_volume_method and the Table 1 bin columns.

Two exclusion rules

Applying the exclusions exactly as the paper states them (prior vasectomy, Y chromosome microdeletion, solitary testis, testosterone or steroid use) gives Miami 943 and Chicago 614, not the paper’s 946 and 596. The paper’s counts are recovered exactly by:

  • not excluding Y microdeletions,

  • reading solitary testis from the absence-reason codes 1 to 3 in Miami (code 4 is kept) and from a zero testis volume in Chicago,

  • defining azoospermia in Chicago as sperm concentration below 0.01 M/mL (three cryptozoospermic rows), rather than exactly zero.

preprocessing.py applies this reproduced rule by default and the literal rule with --exclusions stated. The choice does not change the AUCs or the calibration summaries. Milan (1,955) matches either way.

Things to know

  • Chicago testis volumes are not volumes. The Chicago sheet carries ultrasound long-axis lengths in cm (median 4.5), while Miami and Milan carry orchidometer volumes in mL (median 14 to 15). The paper’s Table 1 reports a Chicago median of 14.8 mL; the conversion it used is not recoverable from the deposit. preprocessing.py labels the method per site and does not convert. The reported Chicago AUC is unaffected (a monotone rescaling of one predictor barely moves discrimination), but the Chicago intercept is: with lengths in cm the model happens to be calibrated-in-the-large, and with volumes in mL it would under-predict by about +0.85 on the log-odds scale.

  • Milan’s FSH bins use Milan’s own cutoff. The Table 1 FSH categories for Milan use high FSH ≥ 12.5 IU/L, not the Miami reference range used for the other sites. add_reference_bins in preprocessing.py uses the paper’s reference ranges for all sites, so one Milan cell differs from the paper.

  • No patient identifier. Rows are semen analyses; men with more than one analysis cannot be identified, so the sensitivity analysis to one row per man is not possible.

  • Outcome definitions differ by sheet. Miami and Chicago carry sperm concentration; Milan carries an explicit azoospermia flag plus obstructive / non-obstructive type.

  • Coefficients are rounded. Table 3 prints one or two significant figures. The refit in Reproducing Tradewell et al. 2022 recovers full precision; the library keeps the published values.
