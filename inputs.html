Input Requirements and Common Pitfalls

Inputs

Input

Argument (column default)

Units and notes

Age

age

years; the development cohort were adult men presenting for infertility evaluation

Follicle-stimulating hormone

fsh

IU/L (equivalently mIU/mL); the only input to the FSH-only model

Luteinizing hormone

lh

IU/L (mIU/mL)

Total testosterone

testosterone

ng/dL; multiply nmol/L by 28.84 to convert

Mean testis volume

testis_volume

mL (cm³), mean of left and right, Prader orchidometer in the development cohort

Reference ranges quoted in the paper, used only for context (the models do not threshold on them): FSH 1.5 to 7.6 IU/L, LH 1.8 to 8.6 IU/L, total testosterone 300 to 1000 ng/dL, mean testis volume 12.5 to 19 mL.

Common pitfalls

Testosterone in nmol/L. The model expects ng/dL. SI values are about 29 times smaller, which makes the testosterone term nearly vanish and raises the predicted risk. Convert with ng/dL = nmol/L × 28.84.

Testis volume by ultrasound. The coefficient was fit to orchidometer volumes (median 15 mL in Miami and Milan). Ultrasound long-axis lengths in cm, or ellipsoid volumes computed from ultrasound dimensions, are on a different scale and shift the linear predictor. The Chicago validation sheet in the public deposit carries lengths in cm (median 4.5), not volumes; see The Data.

Output is a fraction, not a percent. Both functions return 0 to 1. The paper’s FSH-only equation is written in percent (p(%) = 0.133·FSH² − 0.965·FSH + 10.1); the library divides by 100.

FSH above 30 IU/L. The FSH-only quadratic exceeds 100% there. The default clip=True caps it at 1; the logistic model has no such problem.

Rounded coefficients. The logistic coefficients are printed to one or two significant figures. At the extremes of age and testosterone this rounding alone can move the log-odds by several tenths. A refit on the Miami data recovers full-precision values (see Reproducing Tradewell et al. 2022); the library keeps the as-published numbers.

Missing values in cohorts. add_azoospermia_risk_cols returns NaN in azoo_lr for rows missing any of the five inputs. Count them before summarizing, since complete-case results may not represent the full cohort. preprocessing.py median-imputes LH, testosterone and testis volume within site by default (--no-impute to turn that off), which is what the validation numbers in these docs use.
